The idea

What's a Schulte table?

A grid of shuffled numbers that you find in order, as quickly as you can. It's named after the German psychiatrist Walter Schulte, and it has been used for decades to practise attention, visual search and speed reading.

The trick is to stop hunting number by number. Fix your eyes near the center and let the next number come to you. The more you play, the wider your field of attention feels, and the faster your times get.

  1. Look at the centerKeep your gaze steady and use your side vision.
  2. Find the next numberTap it. A miss costs you time, so stay calm.
  3. Beat yesterdayYour time, your pace and your misses show you how you're improving.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

When does 49 come out?

Soon. 49 is in testing now and is coming to the App Store for iPhone. Watch for the release on GitHub to hear the moment it's out.

What's the difference between Normal and Competitive?

Normal is a plain time trial: no effects and no points, just your time. Competitive adds points, combos and a multiplier up to ×3, with effects and sounds that build as you go. Each style has its own records and ranks.

How does scoring work in Competitive?

Each number is worth 100 points plus up to 50 for finding it fast. A combo of 5 doubles your points and a combo of 15 triples them. If the combo timer runs out the combo is lost, and a wrong tap halves it. Numbers slowly lose value as a game goes on, so studying the board before you start never pays.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every mode, board, look and feature is free. Free players see an ad between some games, never during one. 49 Premium removes the ads, and you can also watch one ad for an hour without any.

What do I need to play?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

What is a Schulte table?

A grid of shuffled numbers that you find in order as fast as you can. It's a classic exercise for attention, visual search and speed reading, named after the psychiatrist Walter Schulte.

Will it improve my focus?

You'll see your own times, pace and accuracy improve as you play, and Insights shows you where. 49 is a game for practising focus, not a medical product: it doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

Does it collect my data?

Your games, scores and stats stay on your iPhone, and there's no account. Game Center is optional. The ads in the free version come from an ad network, which may collect some data to show them; Premium removes them. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I restore 49 Premium on a new iPhone?

Open Settings → 49 Premium in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Is there an Android version?

Not at the moment. 49 is made for iPhone.
