Three ways to play

Count up from 1 to 49 or down from 49 to 1. Each mode keeps its own records and its own rank.

Classic

One board, one time. Beat your best, climb from Rookie to Legend and see exactly where the seconds went.

Endless

Finish a board and the next one is already waiting. Your score is your pace in numbers a minute. Stop when you like.

Beat the Clock

The clock runs down. Every number you find gives time back, and every miss takes some away. How far can you go?

Four kinds of board

5 × 5 up to 11 × 11

A quick 5 × 5, the standard 7 × 7, and 9 × 9 or 11 × 11 for tryhards.

Red & Black

Stuck? A hint shows where the next number is. You get a few each day, and each one adds 5 seconds, so hints help you learn but never set a record.

Race your friends

Play 1 v 1, 2 v 2 or with a whole group, from a class to a team at work. Everyone gets exactly the same board, so the only difference between you is focus.

Race
Same board for everyone, round after round. The fastest wins each round, and the points add up to the final standings.
Points battle
Ranked by Competitive points, so speed and combos both count. A clean streak can beat a quicker but messier run.
Knockout
For groups. The slowest player each round is out, until one is left standing.
Ghost race
You play first and your run becomes the ghost. Friends race it whenever they like that day, and see live whether they are ahead or behind.
Steal
One board, live, head to head. Whoever taps a number first scores it, and it disappears for the other player.

Friends join with a four-character room code, a QR code or a link, and everyone picks a nickname for the game. No phone each? Pass yours around in guest mode.

Trained like a racing driver

At racing speed, focus is everything. Professional drivers train attention, visual scanning and reactions as seriously as their fitness, and some use Schulte tables to do it. 49 turns that same drill into a minute a day.

300 km/h

Where one glance has to take in everything around you.

1 minute

A day is enough for the same drill. No race car needed.

A clean time trial.

Just you, the board and the clock. No points and no effects, nothing to pull your eyes away from the numbers. This is the classic Schulte table, and the purest test of your focus.

After each game you see your time, pace and misses, your focus lapses, and your Rhythm: how evenly you found each number.

Points, combos, ×3.

Every number is worth 100 points, plus up to 50 for speed. Find the next one before the combo timer runs out and the multiplier climbs: ×2 at a combo of 5, ×3 at 15.

A wrong tap halves the combo but never takes points away. Want the effects without the pressure? Relax keeps the sounds and celebrations and drops the points.

Why we made 49

All day, apps compete for your attention, and they are very good at winning. Feeds, notifications and autoplay are built to take it. Almost nothing is built to give it back.

We think that is backwards. Focus is what lets you read a page without drifting, study without reaching for your phone and do your best work. Like strength or speed, it is something you can practise.

So we took the Schulte table, a paper drill used for decades to train attention, visual search and speed reading, and made it a game you want to come back to. A minute a game, a new board every day, and after each one a sentence on what got better.

It is a game, not a medical product, and it won’t diagnose or treat anything. What it gives you is one minute a day that trains your focus instead of draining it.

When does 49 come out?

Soon. It is in open beta on TestFlight right now, and the App Store for iPhone comes next.

What do I need to play?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every mode, board, look and feature is free. Free players see an ad between some games, never during one. Premium removes the ads, and one rewarded ad gives you 30 minutes without any.

What is a Schulte table?

A grid of shuffled numbers that you find in order as fast as you can. It is a classic exercise for attention, visual search and speed reading, named after the German psychiatrist Walter Schulte.

What is the difference between Normal and Competitive?

Normal is a plain time trial: no effects and no points, just your time. Competitive adds points, combos and a multiplier up to ×3, with effects and sounds that build as you go. Each style keeps its own records and ranks.

How does scoring work in Competitive?

Each number is worth 100 points plus a speed bonus of up to 50 that shrinks the longer you take. A combo of 5 doubles your points and a combo of 15 triples them. If the combo timer runs out the combo is lost, and a wrong tap halves it. Numbers slowly lose value as a game goes on, so studying the board before you start never pays.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Multiplayer has 1 v 1, 2 v 2 and group rooms with Race, Points battle, Knockout, Ghost race and Steal. Friends join with a code, a QR code or a link. You can also pass your phone around in guest mode.

Will it improve my focus?

You will see your own times, pace and accuracy improve as you play, and Insights shows you where. 49 is a game for practising focus, not a medical product. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

Is it accessible?

Yes. It has full VoiceOver support, Reduce Motion, Bold Text and charts you can hear as Audio Graphs. You can turn off confetti, flashes or haptics in the Accessibility settings.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Polish, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Japanese and Korean.

Does it collect my data?

Your games, scores and stats stay on your iPhone, and there is no account. The app sends anonymous usage statistics with no names and no game history, and you can turn them off in Settings. Game Center is optional. The ads in the free version come from an ad network that may collect some data to show them, and Premium removes them. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I restore Premium on a new iPhone?

In the app, open Settings, then Premium, and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. 49 is made for iPhone for now. Join the Android waiting list and we will email you when it is out.
