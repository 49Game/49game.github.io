Support

We're happy to help.

Contact

Email contact49game@icloud.com with your question, your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days. You can also open an issue on GitHub.

Restore Premium

Open Settings → Premium in the app and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Premium with.

Cancel a subscription

Open your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Premium, and tap Cancel Subscription. Premium stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.

Game Center

Leaderboards need Game Center. Sign in under your iPhone's Settings → Game Center. Your games are saved on your iPhone either way.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your games are stored on your iPhone and move with an iPhone backup or Quick Start transfer. Game Center scores and achievements are kept by Apple.

Change the look, sounds or language

Looks, colors, the app icon and sound styles are in the app's Settings. 49 follows your iPhone's language. To change it just for 49, open your iPhone's Settings → Apps → 49 → Language.

Something isn't working

Tell us what happened at contact49game@icloud.com. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.
