Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who we are

49 - Focus Booster ("49") is made by Artur Pereć in Poland, who is responsible for your data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You can reach us at contact49game@icloud.com.

Your games stay on your iPhone

Your games, scores, statistics, achievements and settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us. We don't run servers and there are no accounts. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Game Center (optional)

If you sign in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple to show leaderboards. Apple handles this data under its own privacy policy. You can play without Game Center.

Purchases

Premium is sold through the App Store. Apple processes your payment, and we never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation of what you've bought, so it can turn Premium on.

Advertising

The free version shows ads between games, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect device identifiers, your approximate location (from your IP address) and information about how you interact with ads. In the EU, the EEA and the UK you are asked for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in the app's Settings. 49 never asks to track you across other apps and websites, so Google can't use your device's advertising identifier and the ads aren't personalized to you. See Google's privacy policy.

Premium removes all ads, and with them this data collection.

Notifications

If you turn on the streak reminder, a notification is scheduled on your iPhone itself. Nothing is sent to us.

Children

49 is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Support

If you contact us, we use your message to help you and keep the conversation only as long as it is useful for that.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to keep the service secure, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from third parties.

Transfers outside the EU

Apple, Google and GitHub may process data outside the European Economic Area. They rely on safeguards recognised under the GDPR, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to how it is used, and to withdraw consent at any time. Just email contact49game@icloud.com. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Poland that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
